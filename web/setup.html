When to check in

Snoozes, meetings and focus sessions protect your time. Only explicitly urgent tasks can interrupt a holiday.

Projects & focus days

Tag work by client or project and by area, so every check-in says what it’s for. Paco discovers projects from repository and issue links, matches specific clues, and learns from your corrections. Your model sorts less obvious tasks and suggests areas when there is enough context. Uncertain matches stay for review. You can always change a task’s project in Today & workspace.

Add a project

Hints are words, email domains, repository paths or issue-key prefixes that only this project’s work contains.

Until the end of today, only the chosen project checks in. Anything you marked urgent still does.

Protect my flow

Uses only the foreground app and time since your last interaction. No screenshots, document text or keystrokes are collected. Activity suggests focus; it does not judge the quality of your work.

App names separated by commas. Add your browser if you use it for work; Paco cannot distinguish work tabs from other tabs.

Manual focus sessions and meetings always stay protected. Long idle periods stay quiet, too.

Call me out

Before interrupting, Paco looks at what you’re doing. After a minute on something that’s clearly not work while you’re behind, he goes red rage and screams. Never during meetings, focus sessions, pauses, holidays or outside your hours. At most every 15 minutes and 8 times a day.

A late task you marked urgent always counts.

Fine-tune (optional)

Apps or sites, separated by commas. Your work apps from Protect my flow are never called out. Games always are.

To recognise sites in a browser, Paco reads the browser’s window title (needs Accessibility permission). It’s used for the decision and never saved. A hosted model only sees it if you allow “What I’m doing” in Model. Call-outs are deleted after a day.
